# Reference-based replacements

Built-in image generation, using the original product photographs as visual references. Replaces the first unreferenced concept set in the website. Source photos remain unchanged.

Final files: assets/photography/green-{robusta,arabica-unwashed,arabica-washed}-reference-{480,960,1440}.webp. PNG masters in assets/photography/masters/ with matching -reference names.

## robusta

Use case: lighting-weather / product photography. Create a high quality realistic export catalogue photograph based very closely on this actual supplied robusta green coffee product photograph. This input is the authoritative product reference. Preserve the actual bean morphology, size distribution, center seams, irregular surfaces, flatter faces and rounded backs, and muted olive khaki color differences seen in the reference. Do not substitute generic stock coffee beans. Improve photography with neutral soft studio daylight, clear natural detail and accurate dry matte surfaces. Show these same kinds of beans filling a shallow plain light wooden sample tray, viewed nearly overhead. Portrait 4:5 composition with beans occupying 90% of frame, subtle wooden tray edge. Restrained warm neutral styling, realistic rather than glamorized. Especially preserve reference-specific wrinkles, uneven seams and flat chunky outlines. No giant smooth elongated beans, no oily sheen, no bright green, no roasted brown beans, no brass scoop, no ceramic bowl, no props, no text, no collage. The goal is the user's real product photographed professionally, not a different coffee product.
