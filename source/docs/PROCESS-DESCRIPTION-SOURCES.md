# Processing copy — 10 November 2026

The animation captions, processing overview and enquiry choices describe three alternative processing routes, not successive treatments of one lot. They avoid guaranteed flavour outcomes and do not imply honey is added.

Primary references consulted:

- [TechnoServe, Coffee Wet Mill Processing Guide](https://www.technoserve.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/TechnoServe-Wet-Mill-Processing-Guide.pdf), pages 3–5: fruit anatomy; pulping; fermentation/mechanical mucilage removal; washed, honey and natural definitions. Used for animation captions.
- [ITC MARKUP II, Coffee Processing Theory student handbook](https://www.eacmarkup.org/images/resources/handbook/ITC-MARKUP-II_Coffee-Processing-theory-CQI-QP2-preparatory-course-student-booklet.pdf), chapters 4–5: fruit layers and the three principal processing methods. Used for the processing overview and enquiry terminology.

The Natural illustration separates the dry fruit and parchment for visual explanation. Commercial dry milling can remove these layers in one operation. Washed processing leaves parchment around the seed during drying; washing does not itself produce hulled green coffee. Honey processing retains some or all mucilage during drying.
